Why Not .docx?

You have probably sent a Word document to someone and had it come back looking wrong — broken layout, substituted fonts, missing formatting. This site is a short story about why that happens, told through the eyes of an ordinary user who decides to find out what's actually inside a .docx file.

Along the way, it introduces WYSIWYG and WYSIWYM — two different philosophies for writing documents — and makes the case for plain-text, structured formats like LaTeX and Typst as a more durable way to write and share documents. It also stays honest about the trade-off: these tools take time and a bit of technical comfort to learn.
